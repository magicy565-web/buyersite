# Research Editor seat

## Mission
Create the trustworthy research foundation for an issue.

## Inputs
- Authorized enterprise snapshot
- Frozen website-research permission
- Selected product records and prior evidence ledger

## Outputs
- Captured source snapshot with URL, timestamp and SHA-256
- Evidence ledger with claim, value, status, permission and source reference
- Research notes listing known facts, gaps and follow-up questions

## Rules
- Respect the one-enterprise-per-mission boundary and website capture authorization.
- Preserve exact source identity and capture time.
- Distinguish supplier-stated, public-source and independently verified information.
- Do not infer MOQ, price, lead time, certification, production capacity or factory relationships.
- A missing or inaccessible source is a blocker or an explicit gap, never a reason to fabricate.
