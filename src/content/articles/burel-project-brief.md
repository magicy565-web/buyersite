## Choose a procurement route

Fabric by the metre suits buyers who arrange fabrication. A finished piece shifts fabrication to the supplier. A bespoke interior solution needs a project brief and installation details. These are procurement routes to assess, not confirmed offers.

## Compare on the same basis

Ask about samples and their cost. For colour-critical work, confirm dye-lot planning. Obtain matching quotation terms for quantity, destination and delivery. Retail and spatial-project return policies differ: request the applicable written terms.
