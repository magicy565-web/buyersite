## Start with the exact fabric

The Silver product page lists wool composition, width and a weight notation. Treat these as supplier statements for this item, rather than a specification for every Burel fabric.

## Before requesting a quote

Confirm the weight unit, usable width, required quantity and intended installation. Trade price, MOQ, stock and project lead time remain unconfirmed.
