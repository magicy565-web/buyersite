# Product thesis

**Buyersite is a publishing system for sourcing decisions, not a page generator.** A buyer finds
an edition because of a buying question, sees exactly what a source states and what it leaves
open, and turns that into an enquiry that carries the context. The edition learns from which
pages lead to real, consented enquiries.

## The buyer's job on every page

1. Understand what this is (category, supplier, product).
2. See what is known — value, status, source, date — and what is not.
3. Know the next step and what it will and will not do.

The Fact Ledger answers 2 on every buyer page; the enquiry hand-off answers 3 ("saved for the
sourcing desk, nothing is sent to a supplier").

## Loop

search/referral → edition page → ledger + sources → enquiry with context → stored receipt →
aggregate feedback → next edition. Growth is measured only by real reads and consented,
persisted enquiries — not by pages published, review scores or build passes.

## Non-goals (this phase)

A new agent OS or OpenMausBot workspace; supplier messaging; unlicensed image collection;
invented prices, MOQs or certifications; order execution; Shopify fulfilment; a model-driven
art director before its contract and fixtures exist (now: `layout-plan.json`).
